Frozen Dead Guy Days App: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Who we are

The Frozen Dead Guy Days app is operated by Bosco Consulting & Productions LLC ("we", "us"), the producer of Frozen Dead Guy Days. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used. Questions: info@frozendeadguydays.com.

What the app collects

Version 2.0 of the app does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms, and it does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

The app saves a few settings on your phone so it works the way you left it: whether you have seen the welcome screen, whether festival reminders are on, and notices you have dismissed. These stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Content the app downloads

To stay up to date, the app downloads the festival’s public content (dates, schedules, photos, sponsors) from frozendeadguydays.com and festival notices from our page on GitHub Pages. These requests contain no personal information. As with any website, the servers that deliver these files (Cloudflare and GitHub) receive your device’s IP address and basic request details; we do not use them to identify you.

Reminders

If you turn on festival reminders, your phone schedules them itself. Nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app (About the App) or in your phone’s Settings.

Links to other services

Buttons for tickets, email sign-ups, partner requests, merchandise, maps, videos and social media open your browser or that service’s app (for example Eventim, frozendeadguydays.com, Fresh Threads, Google Maps, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok). Anything you share there is covered by that service’s privacy policy, not this one.

No advertising or tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell personal information.

Earlier versions of the app

Versions 1.x of the app included an AI chat with Grandpa Bredo: messages you typed or spoke were sent to Anthropic (the Claude AI model) and Speechify (his voice) to produce a reply, and were not used to identify you. Those versions also saved event registrations, waivers, coffin race entries and your starred plan on your phone. Version 2.0 removes these features, and updating the app removes that saved information from it.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions about this policy or your information: info@frozendeadguydays.com.
